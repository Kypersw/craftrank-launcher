Texture packs are here: a new tab lists the most downloaded packs from Modrinth for the version you play. One click installs a pack into that version and switches it on. Skins from your CraftRank profile now show in game: when you are signed in, the launcher plays with your CraftRank account by itself, and it tells you if the skin helper could not be downloaded. From this version on, the launcher also tells you when a newer version is out.
